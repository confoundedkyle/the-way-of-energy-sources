# The Way of Energy source bibliography

*Everybody’s generating. Show me the grid.*

Compiled for Kyle Byrd’s publication. Source-record cutoff: October 29, 2026; bibliography assembled November 5, 2026.

This master register includes **296 distinct documented sources**: **132** in the current 2026 source register, **162** screened but excluded or not selected, and **2** additional editorial context sources. It includes material not discussed in the article’s individual sections.

The references follow APA 7 author–date–title–source–URL order, with italicized titles and alphabetic ordering within each part. Exact authors and dates are used where recoverable; unresolved authors use title-first entries, unresolved dates use *n.d.*, and descriptive or incomplete titles are identified. These conventions do not turn missing citation metadata into verified facts. Quoted executives are not automatically treated as authors.

The 13-category graphics contain 249 supported company-category entries across 780 positions, with 531 unvalidated positions. One publication may support several entries. Those entry counts are not article counts. Removing the feedback and follow-through column did not erase its underlying sources from this bibliography.

The companion discovery log records **1778 encountered URLs**. Search hits, retrieval attempts, full-text inspections, and verified sources are different stages. Temporary signed download parameters are removed from public discovery URLs. The log does not establish that every linked page was fully read; this bibliography is the reconstructable source record, not a claim to recover unlogged browsing history. Older screening decisions retain their historical status and have not all been rechecked.
